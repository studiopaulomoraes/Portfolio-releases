Job Hunter

Job Hunter is a local macOS app that helps you track a remote job search.

It reads job-alert emails and application updates in the Google account you connect, keeps those jobs on your computer, and can email a scan report back to that same account. Fit evaluation uses the AI you configure on your machine (Codex or Antigravity). If Trello is connected, the app updates your board with the job title, company, link, and status.

Job Hunter does not submit applications on its own. Gmail access stays in your local session, and you can revoke it from your Google account.
