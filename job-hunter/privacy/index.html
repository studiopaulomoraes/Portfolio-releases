Job Hunter privacy policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Job Hunter is a desktop app from Probox Studio Technologies Ltda. It runs on the computer of the person who installs it. This page describes how the app accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google account data.

Who is responsible

Probox Studio Technologies Ltda., Curitiba, Paraná, Brazil. Contact: paulo.moraes@proboxstudio.com.br.

Google data the app accesses

With your permission, Job Hunter uses these scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly, to read messages in the inbox.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send, to send email from the connected account.

When reading, the app looks for job-alert emails and messages about applications you already submitted (confirmation, contact, rejection, closed role, or offer). It uses the sender, subject, date, and the excerpt needed to identify the job or the outcome. It reads the connected account address so it knows where to send the scan report.

When sending, the app delivers the scan report only to the connected Gmail account. It does not write to recruiters or to anyone else with this scope.

How the data is used

The data is used to build the job list, compare a job with the professional profile stored on your computer, update an application status, and show the result in the app. Use is limited to those features.

Job Hunter does not use Gmail data for advertising, credit decisions, resale, or training a model of its own. Probox does not have people read this data: processing happens on your machine and in the AI providers you enable, described below.

Where the data is stored

The OAuth token and the local database (jobs, results, and history) stay in files on your Mac, in the app data folder. Probox Studio does not run a server that receives the contents of your inbox.

The data remains while you keep the app installed and the account connected. Deleting the local data folder, or revoking Job Hunter's access in your Google account, stops use of that token. Copies already stored on disk are removed only when you delete those files.

Who the data is shared with

Excerpts of the relevant messages, together with the open applications in the app, may be sent to the AI you configure: Codex, signed in with your ChatGPT account, or Antigravity (Gemini), signed in with your Google account. That transfer happens only to evaluate a job or to match an outcome email to an existing application.

If you connect Trello, the app writes the job title, company, link, and status derived from those messages to your board.

Otherwise, Probox Studio does not sell Gmail content and does not transfer it to other third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Job Hunter's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to revoke access

You can disconnect Job Hunter at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also remove the token from the app data folder.

Changes

If the use of Google data changes, this page is updated and the effective date changes with it. The same URL stays linked on the consent screen and on the Job Hunter page.
